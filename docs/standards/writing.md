# Writing Standard

## Voice

VECTIS documentation is calm, direct, technical, and outcome focused.

State what something does, why it matters, and where its boundary is. Avoid filler and unsupported claims.

## Style

1. Use direct sentences and concrete nouns.
2. Prefer active voice when the actor matters.
3. Explain technical decisions in operational terms.
4. Separate facts, behavior, limitations, and design direction.
5. Use tables when comparison is clearer than extended prose.
6. Use numbered lists when sequence or accountability matters.
7. Keep headings specific.
8. Use VECTIS terminology consistently.

## Prohibited style

Do not use em dashes in prose.

Do not use inflated marketing language or generic trend introductions.

Do not describe behavior the implementation cannot prove.

Do not use transitional product language that makes the repository read like an internal work log.
